Blur Fade

A progressive blur that thickens toward the edges. Click the photo.

February 2025 · React · Tailwind

Ten layers sit on top of the image, each with its own backdrop-filter. Layer n blurs by 2n pixels and is masked with a radial gradient whose clear centre grows by 8% per layer, so the blur stacks up from nothing in the middle to heavy at the rim.

Turning it on just transitions every layer’s backdrop-filter at once. No canvas, no shader.
