The cover is cropped into a circle and pushed up so only the bottom of the disc shows. Playing spins it with a looping Motion rotation and runs the progress bar; pausing snaps both back.

The hub is a few stacked circles with borders and a backdrop blur, and a blurred copy of the cover sits behind everything as the card's background.
