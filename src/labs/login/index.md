Both fields live in one inset panel. The focused one scales up by 2%, turns white and picks up a blue shadow underneath, so it looks lifted out of the panel.

Pressing Sign in marks the password field as wrong: the border, the placeholder and the key icon all turn red through one `--color` variable.
